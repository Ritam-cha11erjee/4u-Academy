Phonics Syllabus

Junior Level: Sounds & Basic Blending (Units 1-3)

  • Foundation of Sounds: Identifying short vowel and consonant sounds through "Roll-a-die" letter games and interactive worksheets focused on beginning sounds.
  • Word Family Mastery: Learning 2-letter blends and mastering CVC (Consonant-Vowel-Consonant) patterns. Students engage in "Build-A-Word" and "Word or Not a Word" activities to distinguish real vocabulary.
  • Consonant Blends & Endings: Introduction to two and three consonant blends (R-Blends like 'br', 'cr'). Includes "Mambo the Monkey" activities for 'mp' sounds and Ludo-style end blend games.
  • Advanced Endings: Practice with double letter endings (ss, ll, ff) and double consonant ending sorts to improve spelling accuracy.
  • Sight Word Literacy: Introduction to the "Hi-5 Word Wall" featuring high-frequency sight words for immediate reading fluency.

Senior Level: Advanced Decoding & Rules (Units 4-6)

  • Complex Digraphs: In-depth study of 'sh', 'ch', 'ph', and 'nk'. Distinguishing between voiced and unvoiced 'th' sounds and exploring the three distinct sounds of 'ch'.
  • Long Vowels & The Magic 'e': Understanding how the silent 'e' changes vowel sounds. Includes "Bone Sorting" activities and 3-letter blend "Slides & Ladders" games.
  • R-Controlled Vowels: Mastering the "Bossy R" (ar, er, ir, or, ur) through targeted worksheets and auditory recognition exercises.
  • Diphthongs & Vowel Teams: Advanced blending of 'oi', 'oy', 'ou', 'ow', 'au', 'aw', and the double 'oo' sound. Includes word sorts and "Color the Bubble" exercises for sound discrimination.
  • Soft & Hard Consonants: Learning the rules for Soft vs. Hard 'C' and 'G' (e.g., Cat vs. City, Goat vs. Giraffe) using memory game worksheets and rule-based sorting.
